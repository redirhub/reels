## The story world (`w44-World`)

- **Frosty Bites** (old): playful, a little clinical. The wordmark is drawn, not typeset: monoline round caps, like a
  clinic sign. The mark is an ice cube with a bite out of the bottom, which is also, unmistakably, a molar. Palette:
  ice `#E9F7FD`, frost `#A5DCF2`, glacier `#5BB3D9`, frost ink `#1E3A4C`. Site: `https://getfrostybites.com`.
- **Velvet Scoop** (new): warmer and smoother. Fraunces (soft axis full) as the one prop typeface, logos only; the
  mark is a pistachio scoop with a soft drip on a cone whose rim is a smile. Palette: cream `#F7EEDF`, pistachio
  `#A9C687`, leaf `#6F8F4F`, cocoa `#5B3A29`. Site: `https://getvelvetscoop.com`.
- **The rename morph:** roots soften into drips, the cube rounds into a scoop, frost warms to pistachio, a cone slides
  in, the rim smiles; the caps loosen into the serif; the domain pill rolls to the new address.
- **The search page, blogs, reviews, bio, maps listing and console** are generic: no third-party name, logo, colours
  or interface copy. Search frames are labelled "Example search".
- **Domains:** `frostybites.com` and `velvetscoop.com` are registered and serve pages, so the film uses
  `getfrostybites.com` and `getvelvetscoop.com` (unregistered on 2026-10-25). See open-questions D-1.
- Shop palettes appear only inside the shops' own sites, logos and props; teal, Signal Red and amber keep their
  meanings, and neither pistachio nor frost reads as teal or product blue.

## Checks run on every board (`stills/`)

```bash
PREFIX=w44- PROP_FONT=Fraunces PROP_FONT_FILE=<fraunces woff2> \
SHOP_HEX="E9F7FD A5DCF2 5BB3D9 1E3A4C F7EEDF A9C687 6F8F4F 5B3A29" \
  python3 scripts/yt/keyframes-check.py <video>/style-canvas/project <video>/style-canvas/stills
```

The checker gained three options for a story world (W44): one prop typeface allowed only inside `[data-logo]`
(Fraunces, from Google Fonts), the shop palettes allowed only inside `[data-shop]`/`[data-logo]`, and boards that
aren't 1920 × 1080 (the World sheet) skip the safe-area and ink checks. Two product colours from the Website Migration
prototype joined the token list (`#FEF0C7` Skip badge, `#079455` CSV icon).

- 1920 × 1080; no text outside the 120 px gutters, no text and no ink below y 950; the end card's right half is clear.
- Fonts: Jakarta for the film's words, JetBrains Mono for every domain and URL, Inter only inside product and
  recreated web UI, Fraunces only in the Velvet Scoop logo.
- Colours: every computed colour is a token; shop colours never leave the shop world.
