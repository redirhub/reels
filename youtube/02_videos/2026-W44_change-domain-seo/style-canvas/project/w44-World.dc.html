THE RULES
Inside the frame, it’s still RedirHub
  • Both shops, both logos and both sites are original. Domains are mockups: getfrostybites.com and getvelvetscoop.com (the plain names are registered by others; see open-questions).
  • Shop palettes live only inside the shops’ sites, logos and props. The film around them stays RedirHub: rooms, glass, tokens.
  • Teal still means fixed, Signal Red broken, amber once a frame. Pistachio is a pale yellow-green and frost a pale sky: neither reads as teal or product blue.
  • Search, blogs, reviews, bio, maps and the console are generic: no third-party logos, colours or interface copy.
