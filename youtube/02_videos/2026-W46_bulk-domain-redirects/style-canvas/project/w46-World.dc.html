Two domains carry the story, each always the same white tile in JetBrains Mono. rotawick.com, a retired product, is the one people still type (B), the customer who meets a warning page (C), a row in the file (E) and, in the summary, one of the hostnames connected at last (G). nortwaysoft.com, “Northway without the h”, is the misspelling with 63 visits that had nearly been dropped (F). 1 · ClosedA domain with nowhere safe to go: a thin outline, the padlock unlit.
2 · Open, padlock litThe fix: the arch fills with teal light, the padlock solid. Opens properly.
3 · A knockSomeone came looking: three arcs leave the door. Northway hears it.
Reference only · BrokenRed, the shackle sprung. Not used in any frame: the warning page carries that moment.
Every domain is a door that has to open properly, padlock included; a visit is a knock Northway needs to hear. Born at “Every domain needed two things” (beat 13), paid off in “Hearing the knock” (F). The film then leaves it: the summary and the question are about the method and the viewer’s own list. Drawn, never cartoon: no handle, hinges or wood. The move “knock” means only one thing: someone came looking.

WORLD RULES
What never appears
  • No face or realistic person: a notebook and a browser stand in.
  • No real company’s domain, logo, colour or copy for any registrar, browser or Cloudflare. “Cloudflare” appears only as a plain word, in Connect DNS (the dashboard’s own copy).
  • No dollar figure, price, plan name or free-plan wording. No step labels or numbered steps: the how-to grows out of the story.
  • Northway’s palette and Instrument Serif stay on Northway’s own things. Teal means fixed; Signal Red lives only on the warning page; amber at most once a frame; product blue only in product UI.
  • Nothing tilts, spins or bounces.
