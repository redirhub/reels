THE MOTIF
A door, and a knock
1 · ClosedA name with nowhere safe to go. Dark slab, quiet outline, padlock unlit.
2 · Opening, padlock litThe fix: teal light from its edge, the padlock lit teal. Opens properly.
3 · A knockSomeone came looking: three arcs leave the door. Glomvo hears it.
Only once · BrokenThe warning page in Act C: red light, the shackle sprung. Never elsewhere.
Every name is a door that has to open properly, padlock included; a visit is a knock Glomvo needs to hear. Born on beat 11 (Act D), paid off in Act F (the knocks) and in the closing question (G). Drawn, never cartoon: no handle, hinges or wood. The new move, “knock”, means only one thing: someone came looking.

WORLD RULES
What never appears
  • No face or realistic person. The founder is never drawn: a notebook and a browser stand in.
  • No real logo, colour or copy for any registrar, marketplace, browser, Google or Cloudflare. “Cloudflare” appears once, as a plain word, in Connect DNS.
  • No dollar figure, price, plan name or free-plan wording. A price is a bar.
  • Glomvo’s stone palette and Instrument Serif stay on Glomvo’s own things. Teal means fixed; Signal Red lives only on the warning page; amber at most once a frame; product blue only in product UI.
  • Nothing tilts, spins or bounces.
