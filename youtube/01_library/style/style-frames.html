Three faces, three roles

DISPLAY · PLUS JAKARTA SANSOld domain. Fixed.
UI AND CAPTIONS · INTERYour redirect might be fine.

Every URL and record value is mono. Product screens use Inter, as the real dashboard does.

Layout

  • One stage, centred, on Night. One idea per frame.
  • Eyebrow top-left names where we are: CONCEPT ONE · THE 301 REDIRECT, STEP TWO · UPDATE THE ADDRESS BOOK.
  • Captions sit under the stage in Ink soft; the payoff word turns teal.
  • Tips arrive as a solid card beside the stage, never over the thing they explain.
  • Product UI mirrors the real dashboard. No invented features.

Timing

  • Everything lands on the narrator's word (timeline.ts reads word times).
  • A line stays up at least words ÷ 3 + 1 seconds; renders fail otherwise.
  • Pauses are short: about 1.5 s for a title card, 0.5 s to close an act.
  • End card holds about 3.5 s after the last line.
